Code, tests and tools offered to this repository are taken in under `GPL-3.0-only` only, with the contributor's

SLOOP, and it stays distributable only if every part of it is GPL-compatible.

The pictures in `docs/screens/` are the firmware's own screens, regenerated by `make screens` from the GPL code, and
they go with the code: `GPL-3.0-only`; so does `docs/card.png`, the Firmware Hub's card (the splash, at rest and without its version line), and
`fmhub.json`, the card's text. Samples are not part of this repository; a sample you receive with SkurkyMello
carries its own terms, and without any it is all rights reserved by its maker.

Why this split: GPL is what keeps SkurkyMello from ending up inside a closed firmware (anyone who ships it has to
ship their source), and that is the protection the firmware needs. The original instrument and the design record are
the work itself, and they stay their author's.
